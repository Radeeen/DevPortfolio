## Context

DPR RI works with a large number of partner organisations, and the data
describing them was spread across spreadsheets in formats built for storage
rather than for reading. Meeting participants needed to see patterns in that
data, not scroll through it.

## My role

Solo: cleaning the source datasets, deciding what each audience actually needed
to see, and building the visualisations.

## Approach

Most of the work was preparation rather than charting — reconciling
inconsistent formats and resolving duplicates before anything could be
visualised honestly. I then built Tableau views chosen for the specific
question each meeting needed to answer, rather than one dashboard attempting to
show everything at once.

## Result

Partner data became something a meeting could read at a glance and use to
support a decision, instead of a spreadsheet nobody opened during the session.

> **What I'd do differently.** The refresh was manual — new data meant redoing
> preparation by hand, which is exactly the kind of task that quietly stops
> happening once the person who built it moves on. I would script the cleaning
> stage so the dashboards update from the source without me in the loop.
